The cause that shaped me

In 2022 I was selected to represent Canada on an Operation Smile surgical mission in Asunción, Paraguay. I spent those days with patients and their families before and after surgery, helping coordinate records and keeping people calm on some of the biggest days of their lives.

I came home knowing I wanted to keep building around that feeling. So I did. I've led international student teams at leadership conferences in Lima and Miami, represented Canada on the Global Leadership Council, and co-founded a national council that connects Operation Smile chapters across Canadian campuses.

Today I'm President of Operation Smile McGill. It's where I learned to lead a team, run a campaign and ask people to care about something bigger than themselves. Honestly, it's where I learned most of what I bring to work.
